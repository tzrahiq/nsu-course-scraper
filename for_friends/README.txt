How to install and stay automatically synced with the latest features:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: INSTALLATION (1-CLICK)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click "Setup_NSU_Extension.bat".
2. It will automatically download and create a folder on your Desktop:
   "Desktop\NSU_Course_Extension"
3. It will also place "Update_NSU_Extension.bat" and "AutoSync_NSU_Extension.bat"
   directly on your Desktop.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: LOAD INTO GOOGLE CHROME
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Google Chrome and go to:
   chrome://extensions
2. Turn ON "Developer mode" (toggle switch at top-right corner).
3. Click the "Load unpacked" button (top-left).
4. Browse to your Desktop and choose the "NSU_Course_Extension" folder.
5. That's it! The extension is now active on https://rds4.northsouth.ac.bd/offered_courses.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: HOW REAL-TIME UPDATES WORK
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Option A (Automatic - Recommended):
  Double-click "AutoSync_NSU_Extension.bat" on your Desktop and leave it
  minimized. Whenever the developer pushes new features, your computer
  will automatically download them within 30-60 seconds!

Option B (Manual):
  Whenever an update is published, double-click "Update_NSU_Extension.bat"
  on your Desktop.

In-Browser Notification:
  Whenever you are on the NSU RDS offered courses page, if an update is
  ready, a notification banner will appear at the top:
  "Update Available! [🔄 Reload Extension]"
  Simply click that button to instantly apply the new features!
